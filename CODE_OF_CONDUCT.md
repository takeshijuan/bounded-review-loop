# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Focus critique on evidence and project behavior.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials or private reports.
- Accept maintainer decisions made to protect users and the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact options available on GitHub. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
